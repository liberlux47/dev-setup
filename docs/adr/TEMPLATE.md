## Context

Why does this decision exist? What problem or force is driving it? Include:

- the situation or constraint that triggered the decision;
- any non-negotiable constraints (cost, time, compatibility, team capability);
- alternatives you considered and *why each was rejected* (briefly).

Keep this to a few paragraphs. Write it for someone who has never seen the
conversation.

## Decision

What was chosen, and why. State it in one or two sentences, then expand.

> **Y-Statement** (optional, concise form):
> *In the context of {situation}, we decided {choice}, to achieve {goal},
> accepting {trade-off}.*

Include any scope limits — what this decision does **not** cover.

## Consequences

- **Positive:** what becomes easier or possible because of this decision.
- **Negative:** what becomes harder, or what trade-offs are accepted.
- **Risks:** what might make this decision need revisiting, and what would trigger
  a superseding ADR.

## Confirmation

How will we know the decision is working (or failing)? What to observe during
implementation.
